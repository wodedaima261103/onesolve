What it does

US Paycheck estimates what actually lands in your bank account after federal income tax, state tax, FICA, Medicare, 401(k), HSA and SALT withholding.

Enter gross pay, choose a pay frequency, and select your state — or type a ZIP code to detect the taxing city.

The math runs entirely on your iPhone, with no account, no server and no analytics.

It supports hourly, daily, weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, monthly and annual pay, and each supported tax year can be switched on the home screen.

Every rate traces to a primary source, including IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32 and the 2026 SSA wage base announcement.

Key features

Federal income tax

2026 IRS withholding brackets for single, married filing jointly and head of household.

State income tax

State-specific withholding for all 50 states, including state tax changes effective January 1, 2026.

FICA and Medicare

Social Security wage base, Medicare rate and the 0.9% additional Medicare surtax above the threshold.

Local city tax and ZIP lookup

Type a ZIP code to detect the taxing city, or choose it manually.

Pre-tax deductions

Model 401(k), HSA and the SALT cap impact on taxable income.

Every pay frequency

Hourly, daily, weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, monthly or annual.

PDF export (Pro)

Save or share a clean estimate PDF through the iOS share sheet.

Fully offline

Zero network requests in normal use. Salary numbers never leave the device.

Who it's for / Use cases

Comparing job offers

Check how state and local withholding change your take-home pay.
