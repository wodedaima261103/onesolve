What it does

RockSort is a deliberately minimal daily planner built on two classic ideas: the Ivy Lee method and the Eisenhower Matrix.

You write down at most six tasks, tag each one for importance and urgency, and RockSort sorts them into the order you should actually work.

The first task gets your best energy; everything else can wait until tomorrow.

Everything stays on your iPhone — no account, no ads, no cloud sync.

The core six-task routine is free, with optional Pro features for weekly review and annual goals.

Key features

Weighted Eisenhower sorting

Score every task for importance and urgency; the list orders itself so you stop guessing what to do first.

Six tasks a day, then stop

The list caps at six. A smaller list keeps your attention on the work that matters.

Start with the biggest rock

Finish task #1 before opening #2. Your best energy goes to the most important thing.

Weekly review and sharing

See how the week actually went and share the result in one tap (Pro).

Annual goals

Keep longer-range goals alongside your daily six (Pro).

Local, private and offline

No account, no tracking, no ads. Deleting the app leaves nothing behind.

Who it's for / Use cases

Busy professionals

A short, fixed list that survives meetings, messages and everything else competing for the day.

ADHD-friendly planning

Six slots and a rule-based order reduce decision fatigue and the paralysis of a twenty-item todo list.

Students

Decide the next study task by importance and deadline instead of by mood.

Anyone overwhelmed by tasks

When everything feels urgent, the Eisenhower weighting separates the genuinely important from the merely loud.
