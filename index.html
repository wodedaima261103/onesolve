How we build

One app, one problem

No dashboard, no feed, no secondary features bolted on to justify a subscription. The app does the one job and stops.

Local-first

Your tasks, habits, pay data, notes and memories stay on your device. There is no account to create and no server to trust.

No ads, no tracking

We do not embed ad networks or analytics SDKs. Payment and receipt validation are handled by Apple; some apps use RevenueCat for anonymous purchase status.

Paid once where possible

Seeora, US Paycheck and RockSort offer lifetime unlocks. Jotlet and Echora also offer one-time purchase options alongside optional subscriptions.

For press, directories and creators

Every app has a press-ready page with tagline, short and long descriptions, screenshots and a downloadable icon. Use onesolve@foxmail.com for review copies, promo codes or interviews.
