What it does

Jotlet is a note app that only records. It never asks you to check in, never counts streaks and never nags you.

One large input field sits above the keyboard, with four equal buttons: Text, Voice, Photo and Save.

After saving, the keyboard stays up and the cursor stays in the box, so you can keep going without reopening anything.

Voice is transcribed into the text box before saving, and photos appear as a thumbnail strip with a short note attached.

Search, filter and basic export are free; notes are listed newest first and there is no folder tree to maintain.

Related Thoughts and tag suggestions are computed on-device with Apple's NaturalLanguage framework. Your words are never uploaded.
