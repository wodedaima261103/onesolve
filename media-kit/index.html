RockSort: Six Priorities Daily

Six tasks a day, starting with the most important one.

Icon PNGScreenshots ZIP

Short description (≤120 characters)

Plan six daily priorities with the Ivy Lee method and Eisenhower Matrix. Offline, no account, no ads.

Long description

RockSort is a deliberately minimal daily planner built on two classic ideas: the Ivy Lee method and the Eisenhower Matrix. You write down at most six tasks, tag each one for importance and urgency, and RockSort sorts them into the order you should actually work. The first task gets your best energy; everything else can wait until tomorrow. Everything stays on your iPhone — no account, no ads, no cloud sync. The core six-task routine is free, with optional Pro features for weekly review and annual goals.

Pricing statement

Monthly and Yearly Pro include a 7-day free trial. Prices are shown in USD; local App Store pricing may differ.

Seeora

See yourself getting better.

Icon PNGScreenshots ZIP

Short description (≤120 characters)

A quiet habit tracker for daily routines, streaks, backfill and rest days — no guilt, no ads, no account.

Long description

Seeora turns the habits you want to build into small daily actions you can complete without thinking. Check in once a day, backfill a missed day, and mark rest days so your streak stays intact when life gets in the way. A heat map and streak stats show progress without push notifications, red badges or guilt. Everything is stored on your device: no account, no ads, no analytics. The free plan covers up to five habits with check-in, backfill, rest days, streaks and the heat map.

Pricing statement

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. A 7-day Pro preview can be claimed manually inside the app.

US Paycheck

See your real take-home pay in seconds.

Icon PNGScreenshots ZIP

Short description (≤120 characters)

Estimate take-home pay for all 50 states with federal, state, FICA, Medicare and local city tax — on-device.

Long description

US Paycheck estimates what actually lands in your bank account after federal income tax, state tax, FICA, Medicare, 401(k), HSA and SALT withholding. Enter gross pay, choose a pay frequency, and select your state — or type a ZIP code to detect the taxing city. The math runs entirely on your iPhone, with no account, no server and no analytics. It supports hourly, daily, weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, monthly and annual pay, and each supported tax year can be switched on the home screen. Every rate traces to a primary source, including IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32 and the 2026 SSA wage base announcement.

Pricing statement

One-time purchase. No subscription and no renewal. Estimates only — not tax advice.

Jotlet

Two seconds to get the thought out.

Icon PNGScreenshots ZIP

Short description (≤120 characters)

Capture text, voice and photos in seconds. On-device notes with no folders, no streaks and no account.

Long description

Jotlet is a note app that only records. It never asks you to check in, never counts streaks and never nags you. One large input field sits above the keyboard, with four equal buttons: Text, Voice, Photo and Save. After saving, the keyboard stays up and the cursor stays in the box, so you can keep going without reopening anything. Voice is transcribed into the text box before saving, and photos appear as a thumbnail strip with a short note attached. Search, filter and basic export are free; notes are listed newest first and there is no folder tree to maintain. Related Thoughts and tag suggestions are computed on-device with Apple's NaturalLanguage framework. Your words are never uploaded.

Pricing statement

No free trial. The free plan is a fully working app with a 30-note cap; Pro is optional.

Echora

Time brings back what matters.

Icon PNGScreenshots ZIP

Short description (≤120 characters)

A local-first memory keeper: write once, and it returns on the day you chose, with its own arrival ritual.

Long description

Echora is a local-first memory keeper for the things you do not want to lose. A line, a photo or a voice letter is written once and delivered on the day you chose, with a small ritual of its own. Three kinds of time shape how it returns: Cherish arrives on a date, Nourish comes back on a cycle, and Await counts down to something you long for. Widgets keep the next arrival visible on the Home Screen, and an optional iCloud backup can live in your own private container. There is no account, no ads and no analytics; your entries, photos and voice letters stay on your device unless you enable your own iCloud backup.

Pricing statement

Monthly and Yearly include a 7-day free trial — cancel anytime. Lifetime is one payment and never renews.
