Tagline: See yourself getting better.
Short description (105 chars): A quiet habit tracker for daily routines, streaks, backfill and rest days — no guilt, no ads, no account.

Long description:
Seeora turns the habits you want to build into small daily actions you can complete without thinking. Check in once a day, backfill a missed day, and mark rest days so your streak stays intact when life gets in the way. A heat map and streak stats show progress without push notifications, red badges or guilt. Everything is stored on your device: no account, no ads, no analytics. The free plan covers up to five habits with check-in, backfill, rest days, streaks and the heat map.

Pricing: Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. A 7-day Pro preview can be claimed manually inside the app.
Plans: Free $0 Forever; Pro lifetime $4.99 one-time
