Privacy

Privacy Policy — OneSolve Studio

The short version: our apps are local-first, need no account, and do not include ad or analytics SDKs.

Last updated: 2026-11-04

Common principles

OneSolve Studio builds iOS apps that keep working data on the device. We do not operate a user account system or a content server for RockSort, Seeora, US Paycheck, Jotlet or Echora.

  • Local storage. Tasks, habits, paycheck inputs, notes and memories are stored in the app's local container on your iPhone.
  • No analytics or ads. The apps do not embed advertising networks or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Apple purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card or billing details.
  • RevenueCat. RockSort, Seeora, Jotlet and Echora use RevenueCat to validate purchase status anonymously. RevenueCat receives an app-generated anonymous user ID and purchase/receipt status, not your name, email or app content. US Paycheck validates purchases locally with StoreKit and does not use RevenueCat.
  • iCloud. Only Echora offers an optional iCloud backup. When enabled, the backup is written to your own private iCloud container; the developer cannot read it. All other apps do not upload content to iCloud.
  • No tracking across apps. We do not use the advertising identifier or request App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • This website. The site sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or social-embed scripts. It is static HTML, CSS and images served by GitHub Pages.
  • Data controller. OneSolve is operated by Xinhua Zhang, an individual developer. Contact: onesolve@foxmail.com.

Deleting your data

  • RockSort: delete the app. Local tasks are removed with the app container.
  • Seeora: delete habits inside the app, or delete the app to remove the local database.
  • US Paycheck: inputs are not persisted on a server; deleting the app removes local state.
  • Jotlet: delete notes inside the app, or delete the app to remove local notes.
  • Echora: Settings → Delete all data erases memories, voice letters, mood entries, milestones, scheduled reminders and Echora backup files. Files you exported yourself (photos in your library, JSON backups in Files) are yours and are not touched.

GDPR and CCPA rights

If you are in the EEA, UK or California, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, and to object to certain processing. Because the apps do not run an account server, the practical way to exercise these rights is to use the in-app delete controls or delete the app; we hold no server-side copy to retrieve. Where RevenueCat processes anonymous purchase status, you can contact us and we will pass on a deletion request to the extent the anonymous record can be identified.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA. We do not use personal data for targeted advertising. If you believe a request was not handled correctly, contact us first so we can resolve it.

Requests and questions

Email onesolve@foxmail.com with the app name and your request. We do not have a server-side account to look up, so most deletion requests are completed by deleting the app or using the in-app delete control.
