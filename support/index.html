Support

Support & FAQ

Answers for the five OneSolve apps, plus a direct email route when something is wrong.

onesolve@foxmail.com

Before you email

  • Include the app name, iOS version and device model.
  • For purchase issues, confirm you are signed in with the Apple ID that bought Pro.
  • For data questions, check the per-app privacy section linked below.
  • We cannot look up your app data: the apps do not run an account server.

Per-app help

RockSort

Where is my data stored?
Tasks are stored locally on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud sync.
Is there a free plan?
Yes. The six-task daily list, basic weighted sorting and local storage are free forever.
What does Pro add?
Advanced sorting options, weekly review and sharing, and annual goals.
Is there a free trial?
Pro monthly and Pro yearly include a 7-day free trial. Pro lifetime does not.
Does it sync between devices?
No. RockSort has no account or cloud sync, so each device keeps its own list.
How do I restore a purchase?
Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. The purchase is tied to the Apple ID that bought it.
How do I delete my data?
Delete the app. Local tasks are removed with the app container; there is no server-side account to delete.
Which iOS version is required?
The App Store listing will show the exact minimum iOS version when the app is released.

Seeora

How many habits are free?
The free plan covers up to five habits, including check-in, backfill, rest days, streaks and the heat map.
What does Pro unlock?
Unlimited habits, achievement card export as an image, and data export. Pro is a one-time $4.99 purchase.
Is there a subscription?
No. Seeora Pro is a one-time purchase. A 7-day Pro preview can be claimed manually inside the app.
How do backfill and rest days work?
You can catch up a missed check-in, or mark rest days so a planned break does not break the streak.
Does Seeora send reminders?
Seeora is deliberately quiet and does not rely on push pressure. Use the Home Screen widget to keep today's habits visible.
Does the widget work without opening the app?
Yes. Add a Seeora widget to your Home Screen to see today's habits and progress at a glance.
How do I restore my purchase?
Tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.
How do I delete my data?
Delete individual habits in the app, or delete the app to remove the local database.

US Paycheck

How accurate is the estimate?
The app uses published 2026 federal, state, FICA, Medicare and local rules. It is an estimate, not tax advice; verify with a professional before filing.
Does it support 1099 or self-employment tax?
No. US Paycheck estimates withholding on W-2 style paychecks and is not a 1099 self-employment tax tool.
Does it include local city tax?
Yes, where applicable. Type a ZIP code to detect the taxing city, or choose the city manually.
Does the app upload my salary?
No. It makes zero network requests in normal use, has no account and no analytics.
What does Pro include?
Pro is a one-time $3.99 purchase that removes limits and enables PDF export of estimates.
How do I restore Pro?
Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchase. Apple handles the transaction.
How are new tax years added?
Supported tax years can be switched on the home screen; the app is updated as official figures are published.
How do refunds work?
Refunds are handled by Apple, not by OneSolve. Use Apple's report-a-problem flow for your purchase.

Jotlet

How many notes are free?
The free plan keeps 30 notes on your device. Delete one and a slot opens immediately.
What does Pro unlock?
Unlimited notes, Related Thoughts across your history, up to three custom tags, and unlimited import.
Is there a free trial?
No. The free plan is a fully working app with a 30-note cap; Pro is optional.
How does voice capture work?
Voice is transcribed into the text box so you can review and edit the text before saving.
Are there folders?
No folder tree. Notes are listed newest first, with three fixed tags: Work, Life and Idea.
Is search free?
Yes. Search, filter and basic export are free and fully open.
How do I restore a purchase?
Tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. Purchases are tied to the Apple ID used to buy them.
How do I delete my notes?
Delete notes inside the app, or delete the app to remove the local database.

Echora

Does Echora use iCloud?
iCloud backup is optional and off by default. When enabled, the backup is written to your own private iCloud container; the developer cannot read it.
How do I delete everything?
Settings → Delete all data erases memories, voice letters, mood entries, milestones, scheduled reminders and Echora backup files.
Is there a free trial?
Pro monthly and Pro yearly include a 7-day free trial. Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no trial.
What does Lifetime include?
All Pro features for a one-time $49.99 purchase, with no subscription and no renewal.
Are there widgets?
Yes. Home Screen widgets show the next arrival, a countdown or a piece of your past.
How do reminders work?
Echora schedules local notifications on your device for the dates and cycles you set.
How do I restore a purchase?
Tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.
Where are my memories stored?
On your device by default. Echora has no account and no developer-operated content server.
