What it does

Seeora turns the habits you want to build into small daily actions you can complete without thinking.

Check in once a day, backfill a missed day, and mark rest days so your streak stays intact when life gets in the way.

A heat map and streak stats show progress without push notifications, red badges or guilt.

Everything is stored on your device: no account, no ads, no analytics.

The free plan covers up to five habits with check-in, backfill, rest days, streaks and the heat map.

Key features

Daily check-in and streaks

Record each small commitment and watch streaks and a heat map add up automatically.

Backfill missed days

Missed yesterday? Catch up so one off day does not erase the work you already did.

Rest days

Schedule rest days without breaking the streak. You set the rhythm, not the app.

Home Screen widget

See today's habits and progress without opening the app.

Achievement cards

Turn milestones into shareable images (Pro).

Data export

Take your history with you (Pro).

Local-first privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics. Data stays on your iPhone.

Who it's for / Use cases

People with ADHD

Short check-ins and visible progress replace nagging reminders and all-or-nothing streak anxiety.

Building a first routine

Start with one or two habits and let the heat map make consistency visible.

Recovering from streak guilt

Backfill and rest days let a missed day be a data point, not a failure.

Anyone who dislikes noisy apps

No push pressure, no social feed, no engagement mechanics.
