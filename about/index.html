About

OneSolve Studio — small apps, deliberately.

We are an independent software studio. Our rule is simple: one app should solve one problem, and then stop.

Why we exist

Most utility apps grow into platforms: a feed, an account, a subscription, a dozen settings you never asked for. OneSolve started from the opposite direction — a short list of focused iOS tools that do one job well.

Each app is built around a single question. RockSort asks what to do first today. Seeora asks which small habit you kept. US Paycheck asks what actually lands in your bank account. Jotlet asks how fast a thought can be captured. Echora asks how a memory should return.

What we will not do

  • No advertising networks and no third-party analytics.
  • No account requirement for core features.
  • No selling or sharing personal data.
  • No invented ratings, download counts or reviews.
  • No feature bloat to justify a subscription.

Who runs OneSolve

OneSolve Studio is run by Xinhua Zhang, an individual iOS developer. The studio is intentionally small: there is no marketing department, no growth team and no analytics pipeline. Apps are built, tested and supported directly by the developer.
