What it does

A local-first memory keeper: write once, and it returns on the day you chose, with its own arrival ritual.

Echora is a local-first memory keeper for the things you do not want to lose.

A line, a photo or a voice letter is written once and delivered on the day you chose, with a small ritual of its own.

Three kinds of time shape how it returns: Cherish arrives on a date, Nourish comes back on a cycle, and Await counts down to something you long for.

Widgets keep the next arrival visible on the Home Screen, and an optional iCloud backup can live in your own private container.

There is no account, no ads and no analytics; your entries, photos and voice letters stay on your device unless you enable your own iCloud backup.

Key features

Three kinds of time

Cherish, Nourish and Await cover one-off memories, recurring reflections and countdowns.

Arrival ritual

Full-screen light, motion and a line written for the moment, with four levels of ceremony intensity.

Home Screen widgets

See the next arrival, a countdown or a piece of your past without opening the app.

Local-first by design

No account, no ads, no analytics; entries and voice letters stay on the device.

Optional iCloud backup

Backups can be written to your own private iCloud container, which the developer cannot read.

Export and import

Export a JSON backup and restore it later from Settings.

Delete everything

One tap in Settings erases every memory, voice letter, mood entry and scheduled reminder.
