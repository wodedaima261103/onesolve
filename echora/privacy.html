In short: your memories stay on your device

一句话总结：你的记忆保存在你自己的设备上

Echora is a local-first memory keeper. The entries, photos, voice letters and mood check-ins you record are stored on your own device. The app requires no account. We use RevenueCat to process in-app purchases, which receives limited anonymous purchase information.

Echora 是一款「本地优先」的记忆应用。你记录的条目、照片、语音信与心情打卡，都保存在你自己的设备上。本应用不需要注册账号。我们使用第三方内购服务 RevenueCat 处理购买，它会接收有限的匿名购买信息。

1. What we collect — and what we do not

  • Your content stays local. Memory entries, photos, voice letters and mood check-ins are stored only in the App's local sandbox. We operate no server for this content and cannot read it.
  • No account is required. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or health data.
  • Reminders are local. Notifications are queued by iOS on your device. No push server.
  • No advertising or tracking. The App contains no third-party ad SDKs and no analytics SDKs.

2. iCloud Backup (Optional)

If you turn on iCloud Backup inside Echora's settings, the App writes a timestamped backup file (named Echora_YYYYMMDDHHmmss.seebackup) into your own iCloud Drive. Each backup creates a new file. This transfer happens directly between your device and Apple's iCloud under your Apple ID. We have no access to it.

如果开启 iCloud 备份，应用会把一个带有时间戳的备份文件（命名为 Echora_YYYYMMDDHHmmss.seebackup）写入你自己的 iCloud 云盘。每次备份都会创建一个新文件。该传输由你与 Apple 直接完成，开发者无法读取。

3. Third-party Service (RevenueCat)

We use RevenueCat for purchase validation. RevenueCat receives an anonymous App User ID (which does not identify you personally), purchase history (product ID, purchase date, entitlement status), and basic device context (OS version, app version, locale). This data is used strictly for unlocking Pro features, restoring purchases, and anti-fraud checks.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local content: You can delete an entry at any time within the App. Uninstalling the App removes all local data from your device. We cannot recover it for you.
  • Delete all data: Settings → Data Security → "Delete all data" erases every memory, voice letter, mood check-in and milestone stored on this device, cancels all scheduled reminders, deletes the backup files the App wrote (in the App folder and in your own iCloud Drive) and resets the App to an empty state. Your language, theme, icon style and any Pro purchase are kept. This cannot be undone.
  • iCloud Backups: If you have backed up data to your iCloud Drive, you can delete those backup files directly via the iOS "Files" app, or all at once with "Delete all data" in Settings.

本地内容：你可以随时在 App 内删除单条记录，卸载 App 会删除设备上的全部本地数据，开发者无法为你恢复。

删除全部数据：设置 → 数据安全 →「删除全部数据」会清除本机保存的全部记忆、语音信、心情打卡与里程碑，取消所有已排程的提醒，删除 App 写出的备份文件（App 目录内的与你自己 iCloud 云盘内的），并把 App 恢复到空白状态。语言、主题、图标风格与已购专业版权益保持不变，此操作不可撤销。

iCloud 备份：你可以在 iOS「文件」App 里逐个删除备份文件，也可以用设置里的「删除全部数据」一次清空。
