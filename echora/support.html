Frequently Asked Questions

常见问题

Q: Where is my data stored? Can it be lost?

Q：我的数据存在哪里？会丢失吗？

All memories, photos, voice letters and mood check-ins are stored locally on your device and never uploaded to a server. Uninstalling the App deletes local data. We highly recommend using the built-in iCloud Backup or exporting JSON backups regularly.

所有记忆、照片、语音信与心情打卡只存在你的设备本地。卸载应用会删除本地数据。建议开启内置的 iCloud 备份，或定期导出 JSON 备份。

Q: How does the iCloud Backup work?

Q：iCloud 备份是怎么运作的？

When you enable it in Settings, Echora writes a timestamped backup file into your own iCloud Drive. Each backup creates a new file. You can see and delete these files in your iOS "Files" app at any time. We do not have access to them.

当你在设置中开启后，Echora 会向你的 iCloud 云盘写入带时间戳的备份文件。每次备份都会生成一个新文件。你可以随时在 iOS 的「文件」App 中查看或删除它们，我们无法读取。

Q: How do I delete all my data?

Q：如何删除我的全部数据？

Open Settings → Data Security → "Delete all data" and confirm. This erases every memory, voice letter, mood check-in and milestone on this device, cancels all scheduled reminders, deletes the backup files Echora wrote (in the app folder and in your own iCloud Drive) and resets the App to an empty state. Your language, theme, icon style and Pro purchase are kept, and it cannot be undone. You can also delete individual entries inside the App, or uninstall the App to remove all local data.

打开「设置 → 数据安全 → 删除全部数据」并确认，即可清除本机保存的全部记忆、语音信、心情打卡与里程碑，取消所有已排程的提醒，删除 Echora 写出的备份文件（App 目录内的与你自己 iCloud 云盘内的），并把 App 恢复到空白状态。语言、主题、图标风格与专业版权益保持不变，此操作不可撤销。你也可以在应用内删除单条记录，或直接卸载应用以清除全部本地数据。

Q: How does the 7-day Pro trial work?

Q：7 天专业版试用怎么算？

The trial applies to Monthly and Yearly subscriptions. It never starts on its own. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before it ends, the subscription renews and charges your Apple ID. The Lifetime purchase is a one-time charge with no trial.

试用仅适用于月付和年付订阅，需手动开启。若未在结束前 24 小时取消，将自动续订扣费。终身买断是一次性付费，不包含试用。

Q: After changing phones, is my purchase still there?

Q：换手机后，专业版还在吗？

Yes. Tap "Restore Purchases" in the membership screen using the same Apple ID. Your local data won't sync automatically unless you restore from an iCloud backup or import a JSON file.

还在。请用同一 Apple ID 在会员页点击「恢复购买」。本地数据不会自动同步，需通过 iCloud 恢复或导入 JSON 文件。
